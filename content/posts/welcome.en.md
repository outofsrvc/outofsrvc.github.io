This is the personal blog of **P01NT**, dedicated to technical content on reverse engineering and everything underneath it.

## Blog Focus

- 🔍 **Reverse Engineering** — Analysis techniques, disassembly tools, research methodologies
- 🛡️ **Cybersecurity** — Malware analysis, vulnerability research, memory exploitation
- ⚙️ **Operating Systems** — Windows Internals, Linux Kernel, memory management
- 💻 **Low-level Programming** — Assembly, C/C++, Rust, performance optimization

## The Entry-P01NT Workshop Now Lives Here

The [**Entry-P01NT workshop**](/en/workshop/) used to be published as a standalone site. It is now a first-class part of this blog: eleven lessons across three stages, two practical labs, and a single curriculum page that holds them in the right order.

That means one search across both articles and lessons, one design, and one link to share.

The distinction between the two still holds in the content itself: the **workshop** is a graded learning path meant to be read in order from `0x1` to `0xB`, while **posts** here are standalone research and analysis that assume no particular order.
